Why This Exists

The work is the evidence.

I can talk about what I know, how I think and how I work. At some point, though, I want the work itself to do some of the talking.

I built this archive to give those pieces somewhere intentional to live. Not as a giant gallery. Not as a collection of everything I have ever touched. I want each piece to have context, a reason for being here and a clear connection to the role I played in making it.

The archive will grow with me. Some projects will leave. Some will become case studies. Some will stay as small pieces of evidence. The point is not to make the archive look full. The point is to make it honest.

01

Curated

I choose work based on relevance, quality, ownership, context and whether it is appropriate to share.

02

03

Respectful

Client trust comes before portfolio visibility. Private work stays private unless there is a responsible reason to share it.

Client Work & Privacy

Showing the work should never mean breaking trust.

A large part of professional work happens inside someone else's business. Some things are safe to show. Some are not. I would rather have a smaller archive with good judgment behind it than a larger archive built by exposing material which should remain private.

PUBLIC ARCHIVE RULE / If a piece contains private information, internal strategy, confidential assets, sensitive customer information or anything I am not comfortable representing publicly, it stays out of the archive.
