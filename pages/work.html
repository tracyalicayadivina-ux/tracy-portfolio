Earlier creative work

Before the current role, I was already making things for real people.

Invitations, product labels, packaging, event graphics and small-business materials gave me early practice with briefs, preferences, revisions and the responsibility of making something another person would actually use.

From invitations to packaging, I learned by doing the work.

Some of these projects were playful, some commercial, some personal. Together they taught me hierarchy, composition, client communication and how visual choices change depending on who the piece is for.

Brand and product thinking

I was already thinking about how a product should look and feel.

Earlier packaging, labels, menus and small-business concepts were where I started connecting visual style with product presentation. They gave me a foundation I later brought into digital brand work.

Design became a way to make an idea feel finished.

Labels and packaging pushed me to think beyond decoration. The design had to carry information, fit the product and still feel recognizable as part of a larger visual direction.
