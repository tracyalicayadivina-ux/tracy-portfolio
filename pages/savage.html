Current day-to-day work

I help keep the brand moving.

Natalie is the founder and face of SAVAGE. My role sits behind much of the day-to-day creative and digital execution: editing, social content, app updates, campaign materials, uploads, organization, launch support and the workflows around them.

The part I enjoy most is seeing how the pieces connect. A workout video becomes an edit. The edit becomes app content. The app content needs a cover, title and placement. The launch needs social assets, captions, an email or automation, and then someone needs to keep the whole thing organized enough to do again next week.

What I handle

I work across the parts between an idea and a finished output.

That range changes depending on what the brand needs that week, but it usually moves across creative direction, content, video, social media, app operations and practical workflow support.

Inside the app

I prepare and organize member-facing content across workouts, challenges, recipes, classes and community areas. That includes the small details people do not always notice when they are right, like the cover, title, description, category, schedule and where the content sits inside the product.

Video editing

Different formats need different editing decisions.

Montage and speed-cut editing are some of the techniques I use most, especially when I want a sequence to feel almost stop-motion: quick, tactile and satisfying to watch. I also edit speaking videos, workouts, vlogs, teaser videos and meditation content, so I keep adjusting pacing, framing, sound and visual emphasis depending on the job of the video.

Content production

Sometimes I am behind the camera too.

Some recent brand photos and clips were filmed or photographed by me using the client's phone. I pay attention to the same things I look for in the edit: framing, light, movement, what detail will read on a small screen, and what will still give me enough material to work with later.

Launch support

A campaign is where the different parts of my role meet.

A challenge launch can move through concept, creative assets, social content, app setup, an email, a ManyChat flow, push notifications and ongoing posting. I enjoy seeing all of those pieces as one connected job rather than isolated tasks.
