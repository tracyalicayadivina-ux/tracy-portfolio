Skill Architecture

I learn quickly. I adapt constantly.

My strongest skill is not a particular platform. It is the ability to enter unfamiliar territory, understand what needs to happen, learn what I need, solve the problem in front of me, and turn the lesson into something useful again.

This is a major reason I have been able to build a multidisciplinary skill set in a relatively short period of time. I treat every action as a learning opportunity. Every task gives me another piece of the bigger picture.

I do not measure growth by how many tools I know. I measure it by how quickly I can understand a new problem and become useful inside it.

Leadership Potential

I don't only want to do the work.

One of the reasons I care about learning deeply is because knowledge becomes more valuable when it helps other people work better. I pay attention to strengths, gaps, communication styles, processes and the places where someone is getting stuck. This is part of how I think about leadership.

01 / ASSESS

See the Strength

I want to understand what someone does well before deciding where they should contribute. Good leadership starts with seeing people accurately.

02 / CONNECT

Connect the Pieces

I look at how one person's work affects another person's work. The goal is a team which understands how its parts fit together.

03 / DEVELOP

Teach the Thinking

I like helping people understand why something works, not only telling them what button to press. Knowledge becomes stronger when it becomes transferable.

04 / IMPROVE

Raise the Standard

Once something works, I look for ways to make it clearer, faster, easier to repeat and stronger in quality.

The best person to lead a system is not always the person who knows the most. It is often the person who knows how to learn, listen, connect people, recognize strengths, solve problems and help everyone else become better at the work.
